# Security

If you have a security concern or question, please don't open a public issue.

Instead, start a **private discussion** or email us directly. Just let us know:

> "I have a security concern or question"

And include your email — we'll reach out to you directly.

**Contact:** [Open a Discussion](https://github.com/malekabdelkader/TypeOwl/discussions) (mark as private if possible)

We take security seriously and will respond promptly.
